The comparison must hold the benefit, price, loss exposure, settlement conditions and maturity constant. It is not valid to assume a token, scarce booking or call option is available for a convenient lower price. Conversely a higher advertised contribution does not improve creator financing when most of it stays locked.

## Explicit economic tests

`model_product_offers.py` executes terminal-payoff identities, a separately transferable package-leg counterexample, an indistinguishable-delivery observation, financing/collateral constraints and CKB/USD stress. `model-results.json` labels every result as **analytical**, not VM/security/customer evidence. The cash-or-token payoff is `max(R,V)`, not `R+V`; issuing a free-standing token and leaving a former owner a usable put can produce a fundamentally different liability.

The model does **not** establish that a provider sells a service for the net at-risk amount, that calls can be purchased, that a market maker will bid, or that a legal revenue claim exists. Those are missing observations, not test assumptions promoted to facts.

## Unsent buyer comparison experiment

Recruit only after separate authorization: at least two independent prospective service providers, ten independent potential paying developers/merchants, two potential settlement/capital operators and two archive suppliers. Exclude Ticoworld-related demos as independent demand. Paid work, funded deposits, solicitation and participant compensation are not authorized now.

For each genuine need, obtain an existing invoice/usage level, budget owner, delivery/cancellation terms and best current alternative. Present equal-benefit alternatives in varied order:

1. Direct at-risk service purchase with retained cash.
2. Ordinary conditional escrow/deposit.
3. Protected transferable reservation with explicit wallet/capital, archive, maturity and counterparty conditions.
4. Pay-per-use/Fiber when it actually meets the same usage and asset requirements.

Ask for price choices at all-in costs, not whether the technology sounds appealing. Record reasons, counteroffers, refusal, intended volume and conditions. Do not ask for funds, signatures or sensitive business data. A conditional nonbinding willingness statement is still weaker than a permitted actual transaction; disclose its status.

Predeclared advancement threshold (research judgment, not statistical market validation): two unrelated providers with a verifiable needed benefit/capacity and feasible funding schedule; at least three independent relevant buyers preferring the protected offer to its best priced alternative at costs covering the proposed service; no undisclosed promise/oracle in the payoff; and supplier/LP/archive terms sufficient for a conservative whole-lifecycle budget. A resale claim additionally needs an eligible independent interested buyer and terms permitting reassignment. Absent those observations, do not build more contract features to compensate.

No interviews, responses, quotes, purchases or preference results are reported as having happened.
