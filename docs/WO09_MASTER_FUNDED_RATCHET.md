# Funded increases: bounded native result and decision

Native evidence merkle-financial-1793667979970 repeats the complete three-owner financial lifecycle with an additional paid-assignment transition. Bob pays Alice 0.01 CKB consideration, contributes 0.001 CKB more to the vault, and pays 0.0001 CKB miner fee. Alice receives the full agreed test consideration. The added vault capital raises Carol's authenticated remaining backing from 100 to 100.001 CKB. New backing and actual vault capacity increase by the same 100,000 shannons. Final redemptions exhaust all 101.101 CKB of resulting obligations and return only the designated cushion. The attempt to increase the advertised backing without the extra received capacity rejects at code 33.

This is an explicitly assigned funded increase, not a universal floor ratchet or revenue magically generated by a sale. Original contribution, remaining backed entitlement and current owner are separate leaf fields. There is no token-unit supply or global pro-rata payout formula in this product. Each right's remaining backing is its enforced shannon entitlement. Sale does not create/destroy that entitlement. Only genuine added capital may increase it. Partial redemptions reduce it by the exact amount paid; other minima remain unchanged. Creator funding entitlement is determined once at activation from actual contribution minus initial backing. Unallocated surplus reserve is zero by design: added capital is assigned immediately to named liabilities, not counted as spendable protocol profit.

Native rule: first assignment leaf changes only owner; additional leaves retain owner/original contribution and gain positive backing. Sequential proofs authenticate all changes; duplicate indices reject. Buyer and seller sign the whole payment, and buyer change covers consideration + added reserve + miner fee. A separate donation mode permits positive funded increases but not resurrection of a fully redeemed right; this mode still needs its own boundary validation beyond the sale-funded case.

The 0.001 CKB example is 10% of the 0.01 CKB consideration, chosen for visibility, NOT a rational fee quote or a return projection. No real buyer/issuer willingness to pay is shown. Revenue allocated to protected backing is no longer spendable operating income.

Executable model: model-funded-ratchet.py. With heterogeneous promised minima 9 and 1 but units 1 and 9, naive pro-rata payouts from backing 10 are 1 and 9, violating the first promise. Exact per-right funded entitlements conserve all backing over six settlement orders with partial redemptions and integer residues. This model is not additional VM evidence.

Decision: retain fixed initial floor as default. Freeze the native explicit funded-increase construction as optional research. STOP an advertised generic rising pro-rata floor until units, allocation rights, rounding, partial settlement and fee willingness have an independently justified product specification. No free surplus arises merely from paying a full backed redemption: capacity and liability fall together.
