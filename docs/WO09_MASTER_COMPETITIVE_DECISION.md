# Competitive decision

TECHNICAL ADVANCE: a narrow native CKB protected-participation lifecycle now runs without Groth16 or a privileged exchange checkpoint operator. Current wallet holders can redeem and trade the actual remaining native-backed entitlement; creator proceeds cannot consume backing. The whole-transaction secp ownership path is real. Two financial representations and independent representation kernels have been executed.

COMMERCIAL UNPROVED. No issuer needs, price discovery, executable maker bids or customer preference evidence is available. The test secondary buyer is a disposable controlled party. No real solicitation or financial operations were performed. Creator finance remains a candidate, not a selected commercial winner.

Against Liquid Cells: this narrow direct market removes the proof/checkpoint and inactivity-window dependency, but sacrifices off-chain batch throughput, retains shared-input contention and requires a funded receiving wallet. Liquid Cells remains a useful fallback for a different activity profile. Against individual Cells: pooled storage can amortize at adequate use, but current code publication is expensive. Individual Cells retain independent UTXOs and simpler ownership/proof availability. Against simple staged escrow or cash retention plus an at-risk grant: the proposed 80% native reserve leaves at most 20% gross development funding, before costs. Transferable protected rights are useful only if buyers value them and creators accept that budget.

Against CoBuild/OTX: these tests use full-body signatures, not flexible range authorization. Against CrowdCell/crowdfunding: credible native refund/pledge mechanisms are prior art; native CKB, a root or a paid transfer is not by itself novelty. Existing E1 official-source research remains attribution/context; current commercial demand and competitor production behavior were not newly independently measured here. No external-security or market-superiority claim is justified.

Further validation plan within future authorization: compare actual creator budget/runway requirements with the spendable fraction; ask buyers which native-denominated floor/maturity they value; obtain real maker quote/spread conditions; account for proof/archive service and code publication funding. No messages to external parties are authorized or sent in this run. Flagship selection requires those observations plus wider security/wallet work.
