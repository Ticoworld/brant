## Why this is not commercial validation

Public grants demonstrate funding for engineering, not a buyer's preference for a refund-floor package. Related-party FiberLatch history and controlled native trades are excluded as independent demand. Public testnet counterparties and a proposed LSP capital budget are useful discovery leads, not commitments to use Trade. Native code does not compel delivery, create token utility, guarantee bids or pay for archiving.

The selected research action is to compare a real **provider-approved scarce reservation** against deposit/escrow/pay-per-use, alongside a separate **earned-balance recovery** comparison against self-custodial channels. Do not blend them into an omnibus offer: financing capital and earned liabilities have different ownership and funding requirements. If neither produces willingness to pay, reject these product theses and preserve the primitive for another substantive financial job.

## Minimum mechanism only if evidence changes

- A2: authenticated unconsumed allocation/terms plus owner-authorized consumption reducing cash fallback; explicit host acceptance/dispute and reassignment rules. A CKB covenant cannot prove arbitrary off-chain quality. A merchant signature is trust, not proof of performance. Existing contract stays frozen.
- B: fully funded exact xUDT identity/quantity and one indivisible cash-or-asset package; atomic paid assignment and mutually exclusive settlement. Model specification exists; no native extension passed. Passive upper expiry remains unresolved where needed.
- C: only a genuinely on-chain narrow condition can be directly enforced. Otherwise name the reviewer/committee/buyer and model refusal/dispute. Do not create a trusted oracle under another name.
- D1: fully backed earned credits, authentic deposits, correct liability assignment, authorized consumption/fees and no hidden creator extraction, with a proof/availability model matching actual payment frequency. The current fixed-80% market is not this adapter.

No candidate currently passes all A–E. The demand/terms gate is the blocker, not compiler resources. More transaction-byte or storage work would not resolve it. No additional native implementation, deployment, paid proving or repetition of completed lifecycle tests was performed in this product-discovery phase. Existing technical results remain qualified as before; quarantined E1 beneficiary/LO-01 work remains unresolved and untouched.
