## One narrow customer gate

Find a provider with an actual scarce, defined service allocation and at least two unrelated potential buyers who already pay for comparable usage. Public discovery leads: Cellora/other CKB data providers and independent compute providers such as Infern; these are not assumed willing participants. FiberLatch is related-party tooling and does not count as an independent provider or buyer. Published testnet report purchases do not establish commercial demand.

Ask the provider for current direct purchase, deposit/escrow and pay-per-use terms; actual capacity/variable cost; immediate and total funding need; cancellation/refund policy; permission to resell unused allocation; beneficiary eligibility; proof/archive responsibilities; and behavior after shutdown. An access token without host acceptance is not delivered capacity. Request no secrets, keys or customer personal data.

Compare a fully specified offer, such as the **hypothetical** 90-day allocation in the payoff report, at the same benefit and loss exposure. Require a provider-backed discount or reservation commitment that the cheaper cash-retention alternative cannot already obtain. Independently ask buyers for their preferred priced option and refusal conditions, not generic enthusiasm. Capture costs of capital, wallet setup, maturity, resale friction and actual likely fallback recovery. Prepare an ordinary escrow version as the control.

Advance only if an unrelated provider can credibly supply the benefit and useful cash schedule, and independent buyers prefer it at an all-in sustainable price. Broaden to the predeclared two-provider/ten-buyer comparison before calling it pilot-justified. These thresholds guide discovery; they are not statistical commercial validation or a financial commitment.

## Alternative customer gate if service reservation fails

With separate authorization, seek a merchant/payment operator with **documented earned balances currently exposed to a custodial withdrawal veto**. Public leads are hosted-balance/mint/payment projects described in the jobs report, not agreed customers. Ask for aggregate balance distribution, payment frequency, settlement delays, worst recent failure/refusal, current withdrawal fee and willingness to pay for unilateral cash recovery. Compare direct merchant Fiber channels, frequent withdrawals, individually funded rights and Liquid Cells under equivalent rights/privacy/availability. A testnet mint is not a commercial balance book.

The primitive is relevant only if the operator can lock full earned liabilities, counterparties want the guarantee, existing solutions do not already meet the need, and archive/privacy/concurrency costs fit. No capital or hosted-balance adapter is built before that observation. Do not retrofit the 80/20 creator grant onto 100%-backed money already owed.
