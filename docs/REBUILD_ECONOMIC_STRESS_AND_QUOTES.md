# WO07/WO08 quote and economic reconstruction

VERIFIED offline exact arithmetic: quote_routes.py models multi-leg native/CEX/destination routes with asset/venue binding, depth consumption, fixed/proportional output fees and delays. Missing fee/depth yields UNKNOWN/refusal, synthetic data cannot silently become observations, and real observation schemas require a timezone-aware timestamp. Plans cannot turn a local age check into consensus quote expiry. No external snapshots or executable observed quotes are invented.

portfolio_stress.py executes 972 parameter/strategy combinations over spreads, rising/falling prices, fees, depth, transfer delays, replenishment limits and three strategies. It tracks native inventory, external CEX inventory, CEX USD, destination USD, in-transit assets, obligations and FIFO cost basis. Hedging fees are capitalized into purchased basis; sale fees reduce realized proceeds. Every trajectory asserts nonnegative/reserved balances, exact lot inventory, and realized+unrealized reconciliation to equity change.

Destination proceeds cannot pay CEX hedge costs without a separately modeled transfer; external hedge inventory cannot serve native customers until actual modeled replenishment completes. Transfers count once in transit and pay an inventory fee. Matching hedge-only and replenishment scenarios demonstrates different delivery capacity. Missing CEX depth/cash is an explicit failure, not synthetic liquidity. Some replenishing strategies still lose under stale quotes and fees; P2P principal netting is not income.

DAO strategy analysis uses actual deposit/redemption values from corrected native run-1793544609163, then varies synthetic elapsed-day capital cost and fees. Gross redemption margin can turn positive or negative after capital costs. The iCKB claim and underlying DAO deposit are never added as independent maker assets; owner-marker return is not interest. These models are scenario research, not real network delay conversion, observed execution or a commercially competitive iCKB product. Published iCKB bots/RFQ rivals can already use similar operations/hedges.

Remaining breadth is explicit: funding/liquidation/default/market-impact/borrow availability and actual executable prices are not proven merely by this suite. Stress inputs are not calibrated observations. Historical scenario/test totals are not reproduced or forced. Core counterexamples and reusable exact accounting mechanisms are preserved for later work orders.
