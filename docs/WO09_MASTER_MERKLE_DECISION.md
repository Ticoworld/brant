# Financial Merkle market: decision after native qualification

Technical: **ADVANCE**, with the configured-policy variant frozen as the preferred bounded/scaled research implementation. Commercial: **UNPROVED**; the isolated protected-cash-only offer is **UNATTRACTIVE** against cash retention absent separately valuable benefits. Product: **COMPLETE BOUNDED RESEARCH DEMONSTRATION**, not a production-qualified financial service or demonstrated liquid market.

The architecture is no longer just a Merkle kernel or financial model. A direct CKB lock/type covenant and real owner secp groups execute actual funded formation, creator financing, partial redemption, paid ownership transfer, subsequent current-owner redemption, refunds, maturity and lawful closure. Complete scaled markets reach zero remaining obligations. This result does not resolve availability, network liveness, commercial demand or external security qualification.

## Twenty decision questions

1. **Complete covenant executed?** Yes. Original three-owner gate and subsequent configured policy/optional-feature lifecycles execute on disposable native CKB 0.210.0. Configured full-scale run has 1,156 committed transactions and 2,238 verified leaf updates across 16/100/1,000 owners. The configured three-owner goal/refund fixture has 14 transactions; optional-feature two-market fixture has 22. Every tested market in those successful runs closes after genuine settlement.
2. **No full-state witness for individual actions?** Yes. Authenticated old/new leaf plus path transforms the current root under a permitted operation. At 1,000, ordinary update payload is 440 bytes, including operation/count/index/records/path. Unrelated holder records are not republished on each action. Initial records and all updates are nevertheless public historical data needed for recovery.
3. **Actual complete size/cycles?** Configured partial redemption is 1,022 bytes / 1,800,126 cycles at 3; 1,086 / 1,874,846 at 16; 1,182 / 1,948,109 at 100; 1,278 / 2,058,451 at 1,000. Cycles include real recipient secp plus covenant lock and type. Configured code is 17,848 bytes, code capacity 17,889 CKB; vault state 184 bytes, cushion 322 CKB. These are native measurements, not reused kernel estimates.
4. **Paid transfer without unrelated records?** Yes. A single owner-changing authenticated leaf, actual seller/buyer signatures, positive real consideration to seller and buyer funding execute atomically. At 1,000: 1,524 bytes / 3,724,711 cycles for ordinary paid assignment. Optional topups require additional authenticated changed leaves and actual added reserve. Rights are native-backed financial records, not ownership of a separately launched token.
5. **Independent current proof reconstruction?** Yes, conditionally on public archival witnesses. Fresh child processes query committed history and latest live vault, recompute all paths/counters and recover buyer ownership without local receipts or seller cooperation. After node restart, closed 1,000-holder market replays 1,015 transactions in about 2.45 seconds. Controlled historical-data withholding fails explicitly. No archive SLA, pruning or reorg guarantee is implied.
6. **Redemption after others change root?** Yes. Native stale-path/current-input rejection and fresh reconstruction are preserved, as are actual successful redemptions after unrelated updates. Old consumed inputs separately fail native resolution. Every scaled claim eventually redeems in the controlled runner; adversarial indefinite root churn is not solved.
7. **Independent users fund one batch?** Yes. Actual signed batches of 3, 16 and 100 contribute into the same vault. Target 1,000 uses ten batches of 100, not a 1,000-input transaction. The explicit 100-update bound rejects 101. Distinct funded inputs, owner identities, change and sequential paths are enforced.
8. **Batch signatures/fees/capital?** Each contributor signs the whole transaction with normal secp. At target 1,000 a 100-person batch has 101 inputs/outputs, 67,624 bytes, 360,655,109 cycles and chosen 100,000-shannon miner fee, paid by the final contributor. Each participant uses an existing funded plain Cell; other contributors pay exactly their recorded amount. This is coordinated whole-body signing, not independent OTX offers or guaranteed inclusion. Missing signatures and duplicated funding reject. The preserved smaller-batch rival trades more bytes/transitions for lower measured total cycles.
9. **Funded revenue increases entitlements safely?** In the bounded optional construction, yes. Actual buyer capital enters the vault separately from seller consideration and miner fee. Exact named-record liabilities rise by received capacity; attempted unfunded increases reject. A real donor also adds capacity. No creator funding or another holder's existing backing is reclassified as revenue.
10. **Is a generic ratchet worth it?** No justified generic formula emerged. Naive pro-rata units can violate heterogeneous individual minima; executable counterexample remains. Fixed initial minima are default. Explicit funded increases are optional flags agreed at creation, not guaranteed profit or a universally rising floor. Their commercial fee willingness and proof-churn effects are unproved.
11. **Results at 3/16/100/1,000?** Complete configured native markets, including all settlements and closure. Three-owner policy fixture actually funds 0.5 CKB after refund/re-enrollment: 0.4 protected / 0.1 creator gross. Scaled fixtures fund 2/12.5/125 CKB: 1.6/10/100 protected and 0.4/2.5/25 creator gross. No 500 CKB test cushion is used as an economic success criterion; actual minimum configured cushion is 322 CKB.
12. **Native vs VM-only vs analytical?** Successful financial scale/lifecycle figures above are native committed transactions, using the real VM and node validation. No separate testtool suite was run for this C contract. Host tree/proof benchmarks and financial-ratio tests are labeled host/model only. 13. **Dominant contention/proof cost?** The single vault serializes state transitions and requires conflicting transactions/signatures to be rebuilt. Local proof computation is modest in the measured bounds; historical availability grows with updates. There is no meaningful public-network contention/load benchmark. Cohort sharding remains a credible rival; bounded independent-market evidence supports its separate-UTXO property, not an unlimited throughput claim.
14. **Code amortization sufficient?** Derived storage advantage exists with enough holders/reused code, not at every market size. Code occupies 17,889 CKB and is unspendable in this deployment fixture. Sharing M markets allocates 17,889/M economically, without making it a spent fee or automatically recoverable capital. Snapshot has lower cold fixed occupied capacity. Exact scenario and rival break-even equations are in the economics report.

Independent Cells avoid shared contention, immutable bitmaps simplify fixed claim proofs but do not implement this mutable paid ownership lifecycle, and smaller cohorts may win coordination. ## Falsified assumptions and revised boundaries

- A full-snapshot-sized 170 CKB vault would suffice for integrated Merkle metadata: false; configured state needs 322.
- A 392-byte kernel proof bounds a financial transaction: false; 1,000-holder financial update payload is 440 and complete redemption 1,278 bytes.
- One large witness always works: false; official secp MAX_WITNESS_SIZE is 32,768 bytes. Signed framing fixes the qualified bounded payload without weakening ownership/root checks.
- Configured 70M transaction-pool cycle threshold must reject a 315M batch: false on actual CKB 0.210.0; verification queues accept the preserved larger transactions with unchanged configuration. No production network fee-throughput conclusion follows.
- Batching more always costs less: false in measured cycles; same-target <=16 batches beat one 100-person batch in cycles, at more bytes/transitions.
- Any positive economic claim can exit under the same fee rules: false at the measured relay-policy dust boundary. Preserve its admitted contribution, script-success/relay-rejected refund and blocked closure. Configured minima/goals/residual rules revise that admission policy, without claiming a future-fee guarantee.
- Paid transfer generates reserve profit automatically: false. Only actual separately funded additions increase backing. A cash claim alone does not create new buyer utility or active secondary liquidity.

## Next decisive gate and stopping rationale

The requested integrated native lifecycle and bounded scale hypotheses are resolved positively within the explicit implementation bounds. Default fixed floor remains the simpler selected policy; optional funded named increases are qualified but no generic ratchet is justified. Continued representation or fee polishing would not establish a customer advantage. Stop extending this architecture as a presumed commercial winner.

The next decision-changing work is a **specific creator-benefit/payoff and demand specification**, compared against retaining protected cash plus an at-risk grant and against existing crowdfunding: creator budget/runway, buyer benefit enforceable beyond cash redemption, actual liquidity/service costs and archive obligations. A different payoff requires its own solvency/native qualification before claims. No WO10 or new product is started here; no outside messages or paid services are authorized. In parallel, independent security/availability review is required before relying on this prototype. These are substantive remaining gates, not passed by these transactions.
