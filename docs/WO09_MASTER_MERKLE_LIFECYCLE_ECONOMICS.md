The test code Cell is unspendable; do not assume its occupied capital is automatically recovered. ## Rival capital and opportunity costs

DERIVED comparison to preserved E1 individual funded-claim encoding: 14,568-byte code, 14,609 CKB code Cell, 150 CKB per claim. The claim Cell itself authorizes its owner; do not automatically charge it an additional 61 CKB ordinary wallet. Configured Merkle cold fixed code+vault is 18,211 versus individual-code 14,609, a 3,602 CKB difference. With existing ordinary wallets common to both scenarios, state savings overcome that difference at 25 rights (`150*N > 3602`). For entirely new holders, Merkle needs 61*N wallets, so incremental saving is 89 per right and cold break-even is 41 rights. If individual-claim code is already deployed but Merkle code is new, the comparable bounds are 122 and 205. If both versions already exist and only state capacity is compared, 322 beats 150*N at 3 rights, or 322+61*N at 4 new holders. These are storage bounds under specific encodings, not equivalent complete launch-product benchmarks or guarantees about every wallet construction.

Full-snapshot code+vault is 15,465+170=15,635 CKB, 2,576 less than configured Merkle cold fixed cost. At three owners snapshot wins occupied capacity and requires less archival reconstruction. At 1,000 holders Merkle ordinary update payload is actually 440 bytes versus a corresponding snapshot's analytically derived 96,192 old/new-state bytes. Complete 1,000-owner snapshot cycles were not measured. Shared-vault contention remains in both. Bounded cohorts trade additional cushions for independent UTXOs. Independent claim Cells cost more state but avoid a shared input and root/history service. Liquid Cells offers different off-chain/checkpoint economics; it has not been transformed into this full financing product for a like-for-like throughput comparison.

For code reuse over M markets, the code-capital allocation is 17,889/M CKB per market, not an actual destruction or guaranteed resale value. At 1,000 holders vault share is 0.322 CKB per right before wallet and code allocation. Nonzero capital duration has an opportunity cost; for a hypothetical annual rate r and duration t years, code funding alone costs `17,889*r*t` CKB. No actual funding rate or archive/LP bid has been obtained. Cushion duration may be indefinite because lawful closure needs all claims settled.

## Financing decision

A protected 0.1 CKB cash right bought for 0.125 CKB, with no separate benefit or upside, competes with keeping 0.1 and granting the creator 0.025. Cash retention avoids this vault's fees, archive dependency, contract and locked-capital costs. Transferability of the cash claim alone does not demonstrate an economic advantage over directly transferring the retained cash. No launched-token/equity/revenue/upside entitlement has been implemented. The creator's 20% gross fraction must cover actual useful work and commercial costs; small test proceeds do not prove that it does.

TECHNICAL ADVANCE as an operatorless authenticated financing/assignment primitive. COMMERCIAL UNPROVED as a product; the isolated protected-cash-only offer is UNATTRACTIVE against cash retention absent separately valued benefits. A real customer advantage requires a specified valuable creator benefit, conditional protection requirement or other differentiated payoff, buyer willingness to fund it and actual liquidity/service bids. Stop optimizing a few shannons of fees as a substitute for that decision gate. No external messages, paid research or live financial activity were authorized or performed.
