## 2. Compare three real trading jobs

**Evidence labels:** VERIFIED means a stated source/observed bounded execution exists; PUBLIC CLAIM means a provider's published statement, not our audit; HYPOTHESIS means willingness, price or behavior remains unverified. No family has verified Trade demand.

### A. Asset creation, price discovery and secondary trading

**Customer/job:** token issuers with an actual asset/community and buyers who want allocation at a defensible price plus a usable resale market. Buyers seek asset utility or speculative upside; makers seek spread/fees after inventory losses. A funded cash claim alone is insufficient.

**Assets/capital:** issuer genuinely issues/supplies the token; buyers supply settlement assets; auction proceeds allocated to the issuer cannot also back redemptions or fund an LP twice. Secondary liquidity needs actual token and quote-asset inventory, not synthetic reserves advertised as redeemable cash.

**Best alternatives:** Uniswap CCA plus v4 secondary liquidity for serious distribution; Pump/PumpSwap for rapid speculative creation and discovery. Official Uniswap reporting describes Aztec's2025 distribution as$60million and over17,000 bidders: a useful example of demand outside CKB, explicitly a provider-reported historical result, not independently measured current market depth. Current CCA documentation already provides gradual clearing, budget/limit bids and failed-launch refunds. Pump publishes creation/migration/trading fees and routes launch success into a trading venue. A CKB auction must beat the complete discovery-to-resale experience, not just contract fees.

**Potential Trade difference:** independently enforceable attached rights plus native secondary settlement, if buyers actually value those rights and the token/claim package can remain bound. The preserved cash-only offer does not supply that difference; no demonstrated useful upside package or post-launch maker exists. WO06 adversarial lessons, clearing and financial-right authorization would be useful after such a package is justified. Token ownership is not automatically ownership of redemption or revenue rights.

**Revenue/entry/exit:** only actual auction/trade/service fees that issuers/buyers accept; no assumed token appreciation or automatic LP yield. Full cost includes acquiring native assets, wallet/storage, issuance/auction code, bidding/claiming, pool seeding, spreads, token delivery and exit/offramp. Actual all-in fees and secondary quotes are unavailable for Trade. Imported participants additionally face new wallet/funding/asset risks. CCA already has distribution and a secondary venue; cheaper byte counts do not overcome absent users.

**Gate:** STOP CANDIDATE for another generic launch curve or cash-only protection wrapper. EVIDENCE NEEDED for a distinct issuer-backed economic package; no such package currently wins. Do not restart the rejected crowdfunding/service thesis. Scaling to thousands would require an existing issuer audience, a valued token and real post-launch inventory; a referral campaign cannot substitute for these.

### B. Native trading, execution and sustainable maker liquidity

**Customer/job:** holders delivering real CKB-native assets, and makers/treasuries replenishing exact inventory. Traders pay to obtain an asset on time at a competitive all-in price; makers earn executed spread or an explicitly paid service premium. Published liquidity services show this is a real global job, not that CKB users currently buy Trade's version.

**Assets/capital:** identified token contracts and externally funded CKB/asset balances. Maker inventory, user liabilities, sponsor capacity and fee reserves are separate. Replenishment must be quoted through actual sellers, minting/redemption or a verified route. Hedging price risk does not deliver tokens.

**Best alternatives:** published A2 and direct existing-wallet RFQ for native delivery; CoBuild OTX for composable intentions; applicable AMMs/Fiber only with verified asset/route liquidity; professional RFQ/CEX for comparable supported assets. GSR and Wintermute publicly offer broad liquidity, execution and reporting; those service claims are not quotes for a particular CKB token.

**Potential Trade difference:** shared-capital/key-only access for frequent small owners, or paid allocated execution rights if a customer pays for firm inventory. Prior native pool had higher cycles and cold deployment cost than direct/A2; A2 also allowed sponsor-funded creation. Shared capacity does not itself outperform those alternatives. CoBuild-style intentions and an exact-body pooled signature have different coordination properties.

**Revenue/entry/exit:** realized spread minus inventory carry, acquisition/replenishment, fees and operations; alternatively a separately priced finite availability retainer. A retainer is an expense paid by an identified buyer/issuer, not endogenous yield. User funding, native wallet or shared account, quote, trade, receiving capacity and external exit all belong in the price. Conditional receiving capacity remains conditional after operator failure.

**Gate:** stopped CKB/iCKB maker/A2 adapter stays STOP; no new material evidence warrants another investigation. Latest preserved mainnet snapshots had18fulfilled groups/0remaining matchers,24pool deposits/0wallet-ready deposits. SDK outputs were new-order simulations, not inventory offers. RUSD contract/Cells were verified, but venue403responses did not prove absence of liquidity. Generic new DEX and unlimited persistent-depth promises remain STOP. A fresh non-iCKB inventory job would need its own funded bids/asks and paid customer gap before implementation.

### C. Funded term rights, bounded risk and paid inventory commitments

**Customer/job:** an operator already holding CKB, potentially an Eaglesong miner retaining some mined inventory, wants a defined30-day downside band while retaining some upside and avoiding routine margin management. This desired behavior is a HYPOTHESIS. Official f2pool documentation verifies continuing CKB payouts and a100CKB default payout threshold; it does not verify miners want options or that Trade can reach them economically.

**Rights/assets/capital:** buyer keeps its separately owned CKB and pays a premium; a different writer supplies real capped payoff collateral. No future unmined rewards are treated as available collateral. An illustrative *validation term*, not implemented product, is an oracle-fixed put spread settled in CKB:

`payoff_CKB(S) = min(L, H × min(max(0, 0.9S0 − S), 0.3S0) / S)` for `S>0`, with an explicitly specified `S=0` capped outcome.

H is held CKB, S0 the agreed reference USD/CKB, S the agreed maturity fixing, and L a funded CKB cap. A deep collapse can exhaust L: this does **not** guarantee a dollar floor. Neither a USD unit in a formula nor a publisher signature delivers USD. Both reference source and withholding/fallback outcomes must be acceptable before a buyer can price this right. This exact payoff has not been qualified by the eight earlier risk fixtures.

**Best alternatives:** sell enough CKB for expenses/retain stable assets; hedge with supported perpetuals; obtain a professional OTC option/spread. Selling gives up upside but is simple. Perpetuals add funding, margin and basis; an actual fully funded OTC option may already avoid liquidation and supply the same convex payoff. Generic BTC/ETH options are not exact CKB hedges. No current executable CKB OTC premium was obtained, and no assertion that such options do not exist is justified.

**Potential Trade difference:** native funded settlement and bounded counterparty exposure without exchange custody or routine topups. This must beat the best OTC terms after oracle, collateral, capacity, premium and exit friction; it is not novel merely because it is an option on CKB. Preserved fixed-recipient payoff, event/term binding and lawful recovery are useful starting capabilities. Transferability could enable an early sale only with a willing buyer and separately qualified authorization/liveness; it is not needed to validate the initial customer job.

**Revenue:** only a disclosed origination/settlement fee inside an accepted premium. Writer's premium must cover expected payouts, tail/hedge risk, net capital carry and operations. No price or rate is presumed paid. With independently funded initial writer collateral worth$1000, assumed10% net annual30-day carry alone is$8.22, before expected losses/fees. Underlying income must not be counted twice. Buyer holdings and writer collateral are two different capital sources.

**Paid-liquidity sibling:** a funded consumable inventory quota could serve an issuer/trader who values available execution. GSR/Wintermute show strong service/reporting substitutes. Ordinary A2 time commitment does not qualify a customer SLA: another party/self-trader may consume inventory, and standard since does not enforce upper quote expiry. Native allocated entitlement, independent-customer identity and actual retainer pricing remain unproved. Keep this sibling open; do not build it because the put-spread hypothesis is also unpriced.

**Gate:** EVIDENCE NEEDED for a specific holder/writer pair. No automatic move into derivatives. The strongest next customer case has an observable source of held inventory, but its claimed inconvenience, premium budget and preference over cash sale/OTC remain to be established.

`lifecycle economic cost = entry/funding + executed spread + premium/service fees + transaction/oracle/archive/exit charges + net capital carry(P × occupied/inventory, T)`.

## 5. One decisive next action: paired conditional term-sheet validation

**Prepare and, only after outreach authorization, conduct one matched buyer/writer validation exercise. No implementation now.** Recruit three independently relevant operators holding mined CKB and two professional/custom-risk providers. Seek one common30-day capped downside specification, an indicative all-in buyer maximum and writer minimum, plus best substitute. This is market research, not solicitation, a financial commitment or an instruction to trade. Willing participants may decline or lack exact asset support.

Appropriate real prospect channels, based on public roles rather than assumed demand:

- f2pool's CKB operator/support channel and2Miners' CKB operator channel could identify consenting existing inventory holders. Pool operators are not automatically the holders or buyers; no participant balances or contacts have been harvested.
- GSR's published structured/treasury service and Wintermute's liquidity/OTC business are relevant writer/comparator prospects. Neither has been verified to quote CKB, support this size or want CKB-native collateral. Their public marketing is not evidence of willingness to participate.
- If these institutions' minimums exclude the job, that is a useful result, not permission to assume an anonymous retail LP. An actual identifiable funded writer still has to be found before engineering.

Concise draft buyer questions:

1. What CKB do you already hold, why retain it, and what dated expense/maximum loss matters? Would selling a portion already solve it?
2. What do you actually use today, and what are its complete costs and failure conditions? Can you provide a consented redacted example?
3. For the same term/cap, what maximum all-in premium would you consider, and would native custody matter enough to change that number?
4. What fixing source, capped crash loss, fallback and maturity/early-exit conditions would you accept? Is a dollar output essential?
5. How often would this recur, at what size, and what would make you decline?

Writer questions: would you consider this exact CKB payoff/size; what indicative premium/minimum and expiry apply; what genuine inventory/collateral/hedge and carry fund it; what oracle/fallback/settlement assumptions are required; what underwriting, operations and venue-access costs remain? Request a comparable conventional OTC quote rather than assuming it is unavailable.

Decision rule: EVIDENCE NEEDED remains until an actual holder identifies a repeat problem and a funded writer's all-in minimum, including provider economics and Trade's disclosed fee, fits the holder's maximum **and** beats its best equivalent substitute on a stated valued dimension. A non-binding indication is not executable liquidity; a single matched pair is not validation of thousands of users. If cash sale/OTC dominates, no writer exists, or oracle/exit terms are unacceptable, **STOP this candidate**. Only a surviving priced term sheet justifies the smallest separately scoped technical qualification. No quarantined case is part of that scope.

## 6. Credible expansion, without assuming demand or imposing a ceiling

Real CKB inventory holders are a narrow starting wedge, not a maximum total addressable market. Outside users could arrive through a miner/treasury distribution partner or an asset issuer with an existing community **if** a priced product wins. Those are uncontracted distribution hypotheses. Obtain channel access, actual eligible-holder counts, acquisition cost and repeat use before projecting thousands. Wallet familiarity/CCC support does not supply CKB, fiat onboarding, genuine collateral or native BTC settlement.

Serving BTC/ETH-native exposures would require their exact economically enforceable assets, price sources, counterparties and settlement path; unfinished WO04 bridges cannot be assumed. Established global options/launch platforms likely beat an otherwise identical CKB offering. A CKB-specific payoff without customer demand does not attract outside traders simply because it can execute cheaply.

The unresolved market problem is **which real participant will pay more than inventory, risk, funding, capacity, distribution and operating costs for a guarantee materially better than their present substitute**. That question remains open across all three families. Preserve launch/spot/rights alternatives and every technical fallback; do not manufacture a product winner from this gate.
