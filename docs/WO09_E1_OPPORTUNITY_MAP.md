# Customer and opportunity map — initial hypotheses

No customer interviews, commitments or willingness-to-pay evidence have been obtained. Published competitor products are evidence of alternatives, not demand for Trade. All candidate revenue and premium inputs are MARKET UNKNOWN until independently sourced.

| Customer/job | Owned asset/right and needed outcome | Best plausible alternative | Counterparty/incentive and committed capital | Potential CKB value / decisive uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Issuer raising capital while buyers fear abuse | Buyers want upside with bounded principal exposure; issuer needs usable proceeds | Ordinary cash retention plus risky contribution; CCA/refundable escrow; reserve-backed launch | Issuer receives only unprotected tranche; buyers fund backing and occupied capacity | Independent cash-backed bearer claims; whether protection leaves enough issuer finance and improves on simple escrow |
| CKB holder protecting a defined term | Native CKB exposure; defined purchasing-power payoff at maturity | Sell to stablecoin, CEX options/futures, fully funded options/DLC | Junior risk seller posts finite collateral and requires premium; oracle fees/capital costs | No intermediate liquidation under explicit capped collateral and event oracle; cannot guarantee USD against arbitrary collapse |
| Speculator seeking bounded leverage | Defined upside/residual claim, maximum posted loss | Options, CEX derivatives, direct CKB holding | Senior funds complementary payoff; all losses bounded by posted collateral | Transferable funded rights rather than open-ended liquidation engine; actual counterparties and oracle trust |
| Owners seeking exchange/exit | CKB/xUDT/DOB or genuine funded claim; correct atomic receipt | Optimized A2, CoBuild OTX, RFQ/CKBSwap | Compatible asset owners; solver needs explicit compensation; residual maker bears inventory risk | Direct wallet settlement/claim transfer; ordinary composition is already prior art |
| Issuer/treasury buying liquidity | Finite execution capacity, specified price/term, known depletion | Professional MM contract, firm RFQ, time-locked A2 | Maker contributes working inventory; issuer prepays service/adverse-selection compensation | Enforce finite capacity, not permanent depth; where does compensation come from without taxing unrelated netting? |
| Holder of a future redeemable claim | Needs liquidity before exercise/maturity | Sell underlying or iCKB where applicable, OTC claim sale/DLC transfer | Secondary buyer funds haircut and takes time/oracle risk | Shared settlement infrastructure across independently funded rights; secondary buyer demand is unknown |

Open discovery: economic rights may need modular settlement and explicit occupied-output budgets more than a new exchange. This is a research question, not a selected product. CKB's native capacity is both collateral and output storage; fees/reserves cannot be called free yield or counted twice.
