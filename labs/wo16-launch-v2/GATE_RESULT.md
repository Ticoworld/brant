## R7 handoff (design only)

Keep the singleton identity and exact xUDT type; replace direct buyer xUDT delivery with a covenant-controlled xUDT custody cell and a singleton Merkle root of owner entitlements. A buy atomically moves quantity from curve inventory into custody and credits the buyer leaf; a sale verifies a domain-separated, nonce-bound, expiry-limited owner signature and Merkle proof, debits that leaf, moves the same quantity back into inventory, and pays the authorized recipient subject to minimum payout and fee rules. Offline means the owner signs an intent, while an operator later submits the atomic transaction. Withdrawal debits a proven owner leaf and transfers exactly that quantity from custody to a holder's external xUDT cell; deposits do the reverse. Across each transition enforce `total supply = curve inventory + custody backing + external xUDT`, and `custody backing = sum of all live entitlement leaves` (including any separately represented pending claims). Root transitions must prove no double spend, replay, unauthorized update or overflow, preserve data availability and recovery, and handle occupied capacity and fees. Root commitments alone do not prove the leaf sum: use a suitable authenticated sum tree or an explicitly verified conservation transition with secure initialization. No extension is implemented or validated here.
