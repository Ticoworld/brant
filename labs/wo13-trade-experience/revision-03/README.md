# Trade identity / revision 03

4 November 2026. Keeps revision02's compact trading structure and develops a distinct Trade/CKB visual identity: forest-and-mint colours, a custom cell-merging mark, Space Grotesk typography, and the official Nervos asset symbol for CKB amounts. No claim of official endorsement or exclusive ownership of green/cell geometry.

Read-only visual references: Nervos, CCC and Ekubo, recorded in `references.json` and screenshots. UTXOSwap could not be loaded through the available connection; that failure is preserved rather than described as an inspected interface. The references inform design principles; no marketing claims, volume or prices were copied into Trade.

The position ledger now shows authenticated rights' backing in the selected market, not a fabricated global portfolio balance. Fully redeemed rights are excluded from the owned-position count. Mint emphasis connects the selected claim to its action panel and net settlement amount. On mobile, selecting a claim moves focus to that claim's controls; selecting somebody else's claim does not enable redemption. No signing or transaction terms change on selection.

Fonts are served locally, with SIL OFL license and source/hash records. The CKB symbol is used only as asset identification; `asset-provenance.json` records its official source. Asset/font additions introduce no tracking requests.

Build/browser/accessibility results and fresh private-native regression evidence are indexed in `result-summary.json`. Presentation fixtures remain explicitly labelled. The original controller, builder, approval guards, covenant and public manifests are unchanged. No public deployment, Pudge transaction, new wallet support, production-security or commercial claim.
